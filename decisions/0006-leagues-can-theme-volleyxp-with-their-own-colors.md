# 0006: Leagues can theme VolleyXP with their own colors

Status: Superseded by 0007 · Date: 2026-11-24

## Context

Leagues might want their own brand colors.

## Options considered

- No theming.
- A brand layer generated from league colors.

## Decision

A brand layer would generate accent ramps from league colors, with contrast correction, loaded at runtime.

## Consequences

- Kept as a record of a reversed decision.
