# 0002: Audit Lock In against a written rubric

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-11-24

## Context

Moving parts from Lock In needs a consistent bar, or the mess moves over too.

## Options considered

- Move everything and clean up later.
- Judge each part ad hoc.
- Write a rubric first, then give each part a verdict and a reason.

## Decision

Every Lock In part gets Keep, Rework or Drop against the rubric in the plan. The first check for every part: can I explain why it exists, and would I choose it again today? Common, widely used tools are preferred over custom ones. The first pass (24 Nov 2026) covered 56 parts: 13 keep, 35 rework, 8 drop.

## Consequences

- Results live in the Audit tab of the inventory sheet.
- Calls that are not obvious get their own record.
